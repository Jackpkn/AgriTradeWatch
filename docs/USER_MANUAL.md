## 1. Introduction

**MandiGo** is a data-driven agricultural platform designed to empower farmers, consumers, and other stakeholders in India's agricultural supply chain. The platform provides:

- **Real-time market prices** for agricultural commodities
- **Crowdsourced price data** from farmers and consumers across the country
- **Digital marketplace** (Digital Thela) to connect buyers and sellers
- **Crop damage reporting** for insurance and compensation claims
- **Price analytics and trends** to make informed buying/selling decisions
- **Interactive maps** to locate markets and view regional prices

MandiGo is available as a mobile application for Android and iOS devices, as well as a web portal at https://mandigo.in.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 System Requirements

**Mobile App:**
- Android 8.0 or higher, OR
- iOS 13.0 or higher
- Active internet connection
- GPS/Location services enabled
- Camera access (for uploading photos)

**Web Portal:**
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari - latest versions)
- Active internet connection

### 2.2 Installing the App

**For Android:**
1. Open the Google Play Store on your device
2. Search for "MandiGo"
3. Tap **Install**
4. Wait for the download to complete
5. Tap **Open** to launch the app

**For iOS:**
1. Open the App Store on your device
2. Search for "MandiGo"
3. Tap **Get** to download
4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
5. Tap **Open** to launch the app

### 2.3 Creating an Account

To use MandiGo, you need to create an account. Follow these steps:

1. **Open the app** and tap **Sign Up** on the login screen

2. **Select your user type:**
   - **Farmer** - If you grow, produce, or sell agricultural products
   - **Consumer** - If you buy agricultural products for personal or business use

3. **Allow location access:**
   - When prompted, tap **Allow** to enable GPS location
   - Location is required for accurate price data and marketplace features
   - Your location helps show relevant market information for your area

4. **Fill in your details:**
   | Field | Description | Required |
   |-------|-------------|----------|
   | Username | A unique identifier for your account | Yes |
   | Email | Your email address for account recovery | Yes |
   | Password | Minimum 6 characters | Yes |
   | Phone Number | 10-digit mobile number | No |

5. **Tap Sign Up** to create your account

6. **Confirmation:** You will see a success message. Proceed to log in with your credentials.

### 2.4 Logging In

1. Open the MandiGo app
2. Enter your **Username**
3. Enter your **Password**
4. Read and accept the **Terms and Conditions** by checking the disclaimer box
5. Tap **Login**

Once logged in, you will be directed to the Home Dashboard.

**Note:** The app will remember your login session. You will remain logged in until you explicitly log out.

### 2.5 Resetting Your Password

If you forget your password:

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the **email address** associated with your account
3. Tap **Submit**
4. Check your email inbox for a password reset link
5. Click the link and follow the instructions to set a new password
6. Return to the app and log in with your new password

## 3. Understanding User Roles

MandiGo has two primary user roles, each with specific features:

### Farmer

Farmers have access to all features, including:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Add Crop Data | Record purchase prices and quantities of crops |
| Report Damaged Crops | Submit damage reports for insurance claims |
| Digital Thela | List produce for sale in the marketplace |
| Price Map | View real-time market prices in your area |
| Statistics | Analyze price trends over time |
| Profile | Manage account settings |

### Consumer

Consumers have access to:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Digital Thela | Browse and purchase produce from farmers |
| Price Map | View real-time market prices |
| Statistics | Analyze price trends |
| Profile | Manage account settings |

**Note:** Consumers cannot add crop data or report damaged crops, as these features are specific to agricultural producers.

## 5. Adding Crop Data

This feature allows farmers to record crop purchase information, contributing to the crowdsourced price database.

### Step-by-Step Guide:

1. From the Home Dashboard, tap **Add Crop Data**

2. **Select Commodity:**
   - Tap the dropdown menu
   - Choose from 40+ available commodities including:
     - **Vegetables:** Onion, Tomato, Potato, Cabbage, Carrot, etc.
     - **Fruits:** Banana, Orange, Pomegranate, Grapes, etc.
     - **Others:** Wheat, Sugarcane, Maize, etc.

3. **Enter Quantity:**
   - Type the quantity purchased
   - Select the unit from the dropdown:
     - Kilogram (kg)
     - Quintal
     - Ton
     - Pieces
     - Bundles

4. **Enter Price:**
   - Type the buying price per unit
   - This is the price you paid per kg/unit

5. **Add Photo (Optional):**
   - Tap the camera icon to take a photo, OR
   - Tap the gallery icon to upload an existing image
   - Photos help verify the quality of the commodity

6. **Verify Location:**
   - Your current GPS location is automatically detected
   - Ensure location services are enabled for accurate data

7. **Tap Submit:**
   - Review your entry
   - Tap **Submit** to save the record

### Tips:
- Submit data regularly to help build accurate market information
- Take clear photos of commodities for better records
- Ensure you are at the actual purchase location when submitting

## 6. Reporting Damaged Crops

Farmers can report crop damage for insurance claims and compensation purposes.

### Step-by-Step Guide:

1. From the Home Dashboard, tap **Report Damaged Crops**

2. **Select Commodity:**
   - Choose the damaged crop from the dropdown list

3. **Enter Damage Quantity:**
   - Type the amount of damage
   - Select the unit:
     - **Acres** - For field damage
     - **Tons** - For harvested crop damage

4. **Select Place of Damage:**
   - **On Field** - If damage occurred in the agricultural field
   - **On Market** - If damage occurred during transport or at market

5. **Enter Damage Date:**
   - Tap the date picker
   - Select the date when the damage occurred

6. **Add Remarks (Optional):**
   - Describe the nature of damage (e.g., "Heavy rainfall caused waterlogging")
   - Include any relevant details for the claim

7. **Upload Photo Evidence:**
   - Tap the camera icon to capture damage photos
   - Clear photos improve claim processing
   - Multiple angles are recommended

8. **Tap Submit:**
   - Review all information
   - Tap **Submit** to file the damage report

### Important Notes:
- Report damage as soon as possible after the incident
- Photo evidence significantly improves claim approval chances
- Keep a copy of your submission for reference

## 7. Digital Thela (Marketplace)

Digital Thela is MandiGo's online marketplace connecting farmers directly with consumers.

### 7.1 Browsing Produce Listings

**For All Users:**

1. From the Home Dashboard, tap **Digital Thela**

2. **View the Map:**
   - All available produce listings appear as markers on the map
   - Your current location is shown with a blue marker

3. **Filter by Commodity:**
   - Tap the filter dropdown at the top
   - Select a specific commodity to see only those listings

4. **View Listing Details:**
   - Tap on any marker to see:
     - Commodity name and variety
     - Quantity available
     - Price per unit
     - Quality level
     - Seller information

5. **Contact the Seller:**
   - Tap on the seller's profile to view contact details
   - Use the phone icon to call directly
   - Arrange purchase and pickup

### 7.2 Listing Your Produce for Sale

**For Farmers Only:**

1. From Digital Thela, tap **Add Produce** (or access from Home Dashboard)

2. **Select Commodity:**
   - Choose the crop you want to sell

3. **Enter Variety Name:**
   - Specify the variety (e.g., "Red Onion", "Hybrid Tomato")

4. **Select Quality Level:**
   - **Surplus** - Standard quality produce
   - **Surplus with Value Addition** - Premium or processed produce

5. **Enter Quantity for Sale:**
   - Specify how much you have available
   - Select the appropriate unit

6. **Set Your Price:**
   - Enter your asking price per unit
   - Consider current market rates when pricing

7. **Add Photo/Video:**
   - Upload clear images of your produce
   - Good photos attract more buyers

8. **Verify Location:**
   - Your GPS location is automatically captured
   - This helps buyers find produce near them

9. **Tap Submit:**
   - Your listing will appear on the marketplace
   - Buyers can now contact you

### Tips for Sellers:
- Use clear, well-lit photos
- Price competitively based on market rates
- Respond promptly to buyer inquiries
- Keep listings updated with accurate quantities

## 8. Interactive Price Map

The Price Map shows real-time commodity prices across different regions.

### Using the Price Map:

1. From the Home Dashboard, tap **Interactive Map**

2. **Select Commodity:**
   - Use the dropdown to choose which commodity's prices to view

3. **Adjust Search Radius:**
   - Use the slider to set the search radius (50m to 50km)
   - A circle on the map shows your search area

4. **View Price Data:**
   The map displays:
   | Metric | Description |
   |--------|-------------|
   | Min Price | Lowest reported price in the area |
   | Max Price | Highest reported price in the area |
   | Modal Price | Most common price |
   | Average Price | Mean of all reported prices |
   | Entry Count | Number of price submissions |

5. **Filter by Date Range:**
   - Tap quick filter buttons: **7 Days**, **30 Days**, **90 Days**
   - Or tap **Custom** to select specific dates

6. **View Separate Data:**
   - Toggle between **Consumer Prices** and **Farmer Prices**
   - Each shows data from the respective user type

7. **Save Favorite Locations:**
   - Tap the star icon to bookmark a location
   - Access favorites from the sidebar for quick navigation

8. **Change Map View:**
   - Toggle between Standard, Satellite, and Hybrid views

### Understanding the Data:
- Prices are crowdsourced from user submissions
- More data points = more accurate information
- Recent data (7 days) is most relevant for current decisions

## 9. Price Statistics & Analytics

View historical price trends and analytics for informed decision-making.

### Using Statistics:

1. From the bottom navigation, tap **Stats**

2. **Select View Type:**
   - **Consumer View** - Shows prices reported by consumers
   - **Farmer View** - Shows prices reported by farmers

3. **Select Commodity:**
   - Use the dropdown to choose a crop

4. **View the Chart:**
   - A line chart displays price trends over time
   - X-axis: Date
   - Y-axis: Price

5. **Analyze Trends:**
   - Identify peak and low price periods
   - Plan buying/selling based on patterns

6. **Refresh Data:**
   - Pull down on the screen to refresh
   - Or tap the refresh icon

### Using Analytics for Decisions:

**For Farmers:**
- Identify the best time to sell
- Compare your prices with market averages
- Track seasonal price variations

**For Consumers:**
- Find the best time to buy
- Compare prices across regions
- Plan bulk purchases during low-price periods

## 12. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions:

#### "Location not available" error
**Solution:**
1. Open your device Settings
2. Go to **Location** or **Privacy > Location Services**
3. Ensure location is turned ON
4. Find MandiGo in the app list
5. Set permission to **Allow While Using App** or **Always**
6. Restart the app

#### Cannot login / "Invalid credentials" error
**Solution:**
1. Verify your username is spelled correctly (case-sensitive)
2. Check your password
3. If forgotten, use **Forgot Password** to reset
4. Ensure you have an internet connection

#### Photos not uploading
**Solution:**
1. Check camera permissions in device settings
2. Ensure adequate storage space on device
3. Try using a smaller/compressed image
4. Check internet connection speed

#### Map not loading
**Solution:**
1. Check internet connectivity
2. Enable location services
3. Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > MandiGo > Clear Cache)
4. Update the app to the latest version

#### App crashes or freezes
**Solution:**
1. Close the app completely
2. Restart your device
3. Update to the latest app version
4. Reinstall if problem persists

#### "Offline" message displayed
**Solution:**
1. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection
2. Toggle airplane mode on and off
3. Move to an area with better connectivity
4. The app will automatically reconnect when online

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: Is MandiGo free to use?**
A: Yes, MandiGo is completely free for all users.

**Q: Do I need to create an account to use the app?**
A: Yes, registration is required to access features and contribute data.

**Q: What data does MandiGo collect?**
A: MandiGo collects location data, price submissions, and produce listings. All data is used to improve market transparency and is handled according to our privacy policy.

### Account

**Q: Can I change my user type (Farmer/Consumer)?**
A: User type is set during registration. Contact support if you need to change it.

**Q: I forgot my username. What should I do?**
A: Try logging in with your registered email address or contact support.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: Each user should have only one account. Multiple accounts may be deactivated.

### Features

**Q: How accurate are the price data?**
A: Prices are crowdsourced from actual users. Accuracy depends on the number of submissions in your area.

**Q: Can I edit or delete my submissions?**
A: Once submitted, data becomes part of the aggregated database. Contact support for corrections.

**Q: Why can't I see the "Add Crop Data" option?**
A: This feature is only available to users registered as Farmers.

**Q: How do I contact a seller on Digital Thela?**
A: Tap on the seller's listing, then tap their profile to view contact information.

### Technical

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: The app requires an internet connection for most features. Limited offline access may show cached data.

**Q: Which devices are supported?**
A: Android 8.0+ and iOS 13.0+ devices are supported.

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Visit Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS) and tap Update.
